# Kodak F-4016: T-MAX 100 Film

Curves from KODAK PROFESSIONAL T-MAX Films, Technical Data F-4016, March 2002 edition
(<https://125px.com/docs/unsorted/kodak/f4016.pdf>, p. 15), retrieved 2026-10-24: D-76,
small tank, 20 °C, 6, 7½ and 10 min, diffuse visual density. Read from the PDF's vector
paths with `tools/datasheet_curves.py` (frame 93.8,84.9,278.2,269.4; −4…1 log H, 0…4 D;
10 min solid, 7½ min dash 3.2, 6 min dash 6.5).

The current sheet (F-4016, Kodak Alaris, Feb 2016,
<https://imaging.kodakalaris.com/sites/default/files/files/products/f4016_TMax_100.pdf>, p. 8)
draws the same three curves, identical within 0.003 D, but a font fault replaced its
legend's times with "PL Q" glyphs; the 2002 legend supplies them. T-Max 100 has not been
reformulated since (T-Max 400 was, in 2007, so its data come from F-4043). Times and the
temperature table are from the current sheet (p. 3).
